About Us

Where Vision
Meets Artistry

B.Design24 Studio is an award-winning architecture and interior design firm based in Surat, Gujarat — led by visionary designer Bheru Jangid.

We specialize in crafting luxurious, detail-oriented residential and commercial spaces that seamlessly blend high-end aesthetics with practical functionality. Every project is a testament to fine craftsmanship, custom-made artistry, and curated materials sourced from around the world.

From private luxury residences and corporate offices to showrooms and hospitality projects, our portfolio spans across Surat, Ahmedabad, Mumbai, Bangalore, and beyond.

How We Work

Our Design Process

01

Discovery & Consultation

We begin with an in-depth consultation to understand your vision, lifestyle, and aspirations for the space.

02

Concept & Design Development

Our team creates detailed concepts, mood boards, 3D visualizations, and material palettes for your approval.

03

Detailed Planning & Documentation
